The Vision
The Problem
The modern professional is running on empty
Corporate life has engineered a state of chronic low-grade exhaustion that is now the default setting for working professionals in India. Days have no edges. Screens dominate mornings and midnights. The circadian rhythm, the body's oldest internal regulatory clock, is quietly overridden.
This is not a failure of willpower. It is a biological crisis. The nervous system stays trapped in sympathetic hyperarousal. No one-hour consultation solves this. No generic weekend getaway provides the physiological stillness needed to truly reset.
While luxury spa tourism and commercial resorts exist, they treat wellness as an ornamental amenity rather than a rigorous clinical protocol overseen by licensed doctors.
Our Answer
Four days of physician-led biological recovery
The Reset Co is an authentic Ayurvedic wellness retreat practice founded and personally directed by two BAMS physicians. We curate intimate closed cohorts in the clean-air sanctuary of Bir Billing, Himachal Pradesh.
Every retreat is exactly 4 days. Every participant receives customized clinical care: localized Panchakarma Basti therapies, specialized Netra Tarpana and Ashchyotana ocular care, classical Shatkarma purification, condition-adaptive yoga and breathwork, nature immersion, and creative mindful workshops like pottery.
Guests depart with restored digestive fire (Agni), regulated sleep cycles, and a tangible Welcome Kit that turns retreat habits into lifelong routines.

Clinical Services, Shatkarma & Schedule
Clinical Procedures
Localized Basti (Kati, Greeva, Nabhi)
Precision warm medicated herbal oil pooling retained within an urad-dough dam. Kati relieves lumbar disc compression; Greeva eases cervical stiffness; Nabhi regulates digestive Agni and metabolic balance.
Netra Tarpana & Ashchyotana (Ocular Care)
Netra Tarpana is a classical therapeutic eye bath where medicated ghee (Triphala Ghrita) is pooled over the orbits, cooling inflamed optic nerves, soothing computer vision syndrome, and restoring ocular lubrication. Ashchyotana provides gentle medicated herbal decoction irrigation to flush debris and calm redness.
Classical Shatkarma Protocol (Day 3)
Yogic internal purifications including Jala Neti (saline nasal irrigation), Kapalabhati (frontal sinus purification), and Trataka (focused gaze). Guests are educated on the anatomy and methodology on Days 1 and 2, with the supervised ritual executed on the morning of Day 3 before Day 4 departure.
Medical Safety Contraindication Screen
Shatkarma is administered ONLY after individual physician clearance. It is strictly omitted in patients with Hypertension (HTN), cardiac vulnerability, advanced age or physical frailty, or acute medical complications. Gentle soothing alternatives (Trataka, gentle Pranayama) are provided instead.
