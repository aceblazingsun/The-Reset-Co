Therapies, Practices & Clinical Disclosures

Doctor-Led Shatkarma (Morning Purification)

Gentle cleansing including Jalaneti (warm isotonic saline nasal rinse), gentle Kapalabhati, and Trataka candle gazing. Conducted jointly by our BAMS doctor and certified yoga faculty.

* Clinical Note: Blood pressure and nasal passages are medically checked prior to kriya. Anyone with acute sinusitis or nasal inflammation receives soothing steam inhalation instead.

Spine-Adaptive Restorative Hatha & Pranayama

Unhurried spinal articulation, diaphragmatic expansion, and deep Yoga Nidra in crisp Himalayan pine air. Zero athletic ego, 100% therapeutic nervous system downregulation.

* Suitability: Adapted for absolute beginners as well as experienced practitioners. Safe for all spinal mobility levels.

Samsarjana Krama Nutrition (Agni Rest)

Day 3 is dedicated to noble silence and warm liquid digestive broths (Manda, Peya, and Vilepi) enriched with cumin and pure A2 cow ghee, allowing the gastrointestinal tract to deeply rest and reset.

* Dietary Guidance: Pre-retreat light meal advisory is provided 2 days before arrival to ensure seamless digestive adaptation.

Tactile Clay Pottery & Forest Grounding

Working with natural clay alongside local Himalayan artisans to anchor somatic attention, paired with noble silence walks through protected pine forest trails.

* Non-Medical Therapy: Somatic grounding to pacify hyperactive Vata and reduce racing thoughts without pharmaceutical sedatives.

Absence of Forced Bahya Basti (Ethical Care)

Because you do not have cervical or lumbar spondylosis, we do not perform external oil reservoirs on your spine. Your pricing reflects pure restorative sanctuary care without unwanted medical markups.

* Value Guarantee: If during consultation you do report localized tension, doctors provide a soothing Marma application at no added charge.
