01. Our Philosophy & The Himalayan Sanctuary

We believe that classical wellness is not a trend, but a tradition. True recovery requires medical oversight, pristine air, and dedicated attention.

The Reset Co. offers immersive, premium wellness programmes designed and delivered by BAMS-qualified Ayurvedic doctors (Dr. Himanshu Bhatt and Dr. Aditya Kaundal). Based in the quiet, spiritually alive valley of Bir Billing, Himachal Pradesh, our retreats are structured to restore physical balance and calm the nervous system.

Exclusivity & Care: To ensure that every guest receives personalized attention, we strictly limit every retreat batch to a maximum of 15 guests. We partner with luxury boutique hotel properties in Bir Billing, combining premium Himalayan lodging with clinical Ayurvedic therapies and daily organic sattvic meals.

02. The Four Pillars of Your Reset

🧘

01. Classical Hatha

Not a modern fitness workout, but Hatha yoga taught as a complete science of breath, alignment, and physical flow to balance your nervous system.

🕯️

02. Vedic Dhyana

Guided Himalayan meditation, candle flame Trataka, and evening Yoga Nidra to down-regulate sympathetic cortisol overload and unlock deep cellular rest.

💧

03. Shatkarma Kriyas

Under full medical supervision, you will practice classical purification kriyas (Neti, Nauli, Kapalabhati) to detoxify the respiratory and digestive tracts.

🌿

04. Ayurvedic Therapy

Includes personalized consultations and traditional treatments like Abhyanga (herbal oil massage) and Shirodhara, customized to your prakriti.
