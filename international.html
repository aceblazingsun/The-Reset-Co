Reaching Bir Billing

The Reset Co. sanctuary is situated in Bir Billing at 1,525 meters altitude in the Kangra Valley, famous for its clean Himalayan pine air and tranquil Tibetan monasteries.

Recommended Flight Route: Fly internationally into New Delhi (DEL), then take an 80-minute domestic connection to Kangra / Dharamshala Airport (DHM). From DHM, our arranged private car transfer brings you comfortably to the retreat in approximately 2 hours.

Overnight Luxury Bus Option: Daily Volvo semi-sleeper services operate directly from New Delhi (Majnu Ka Tila / Kashmere Gate) to Bir (approx. 10–11 hours).

Foreign Cards & Payments

International Guest FAQs

What is the weather like in late November?
Late November in the Kangra Valley features crisp, clear sunny days (18°C–22°C / 65°F–72°F) with panoramic snow-capped Dhauladhar peak views. Evenings are cool (8°C–12°C / 46°F–54°F). We recommend warm layers, a light jacket, and comfortable wool socks for meditation sessions.
Are all sessions and consultations conducted in English?
Yes. All physician consultations, morning Dinacharya instructions, yoga guidance, and pottery sessions are conducted in fluent English.
What visa do I need to visit India?
Most international visitors can easily obtain an Indian e-Tourist Visa online prior to departure (usually issued within 3–4 business days). Ensure your passport has at least 6 months validity.
Can you accommodate Western dietary sensitivities?
Yes. All meals are fresh, Sattvic, vegetarian, and prepared on-site. We easily accommodate gluten-free, vegan, dairy-free, and nut-free requirements with prior notification during your intake.
