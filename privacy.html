Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026 • The Reset Co Sanctuary, Bir Billing

Important Notice Regarding Medical Records

The Reset Co provides wellness retreats and Ayurvedic consultations. We strictly advise all guests not to transmit detailed medical files, diagnostic imaging, or prescriptions through our website reservation or contact forms. All personal health conversations occur directly and confidentially in private 1:1 doctor consultations.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum personal information necessary to coordinate retreat bookings and respond to inquiries. This includes:

  • Contact details: Your name, WhatsApp phone number, and email address.
  • Retreat preferences: Preferred cohort dates, selected retreat suite, and guest count.
  • General wellness goals: Brief non-clinical intentions you choose to share regarding what you hope to experience during the retreat.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information provided through our website or direct communication channels is used strictly for:

  • Responding to retreat availability and scheduling inquiries.
  • Sending booking confirmations, arrival itineraries, and travel support details for Bir Billing.
  • Preparing welcoming accommodations suited to your dietary requirements.

3. Confidentiality and No Third-Party Sales

We value privacy and discretion. We do not sell, rent, lease, or monetize guest personal information to data brokers, marketing networks, or third parties under any circumstances.

4. Browser Storage & Transmission Security

We do not retain sensitive guest health notes in plain browser storage (localStorage). Reservation forms format inquiries for direct email or WhatsApp transmission directly to our retreat coordination desk.

5. Direct Doctor-Guest Consultations

Any clinical assessment, pulse diagnosis (Nadi Pariksha), and personalized herbal guidance conducted at the retreat by our BAMS doctors are held in strict medical confidence in accordance with professional Ayurvedic practice standards.

6. Cookies & Analytics

Our website uses minimal, essential functional cookies to maintain navigation state and preferences. We do not use intrusive cross-site behavioural trackers.

7. Guest Rights: Access, Correction, and Deletion

You have the right to request a summary of the contact details we hold for your booking, request updates to inaccurate details, or request that your contact information be purged from our active contact lists following your retreat.

To submit a request, contact our team at hello@theresetco.in or message us on WhatsApp at +91 78885 40046.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may occasionally update this Privacy Policy to reflect operational improvements or regulatory updates. Any revisions will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
