Privacy & Health Data Protection

Compliance: Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 • Himachal Pradesh, India

1. Physician-Patient Data Confidentiality

The Reset Co. treats all medical questionnaires, pulse diagnostic logs, constitutional dosha assessments, and dietary notes as privileged clinical health data.

Your health information is accessed solely by our licensed founding Vaidyas, Dr. Himanshu Bhatt and Dr. Aditya Kaundal, and is never shared, leased, or disclosed to insurance intermediaries, commercial wellness aggregators, or marketing third parties.

2. What Information We Collect

To ensure personal safety during Shatkarma, Bahya Basti, and yoga sessions, we collect:

  • Contact identification details (name, email address, WhatsApp telephone number).
  • Health disclosures (current blood pressure status, spinal injuries, surgeries, allergies, dietary constraints).
  • Constitution analysis from our optional Prakriti assessment tool.

3. Data Storage & Encryption

All clinical records and consultation notes are stored on encrypted, access-restricted databases adhering to modern data protection standards. We do not maintain unencrypted physical file cabinets.

4. Your Data Rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (DPDP Act), you possess the right to inspect, correct, or request the permanent erasure of your personal health data upon conclusion of your 30-day post-retreat continuity period. Requests can be submitted directly to privacy@thereset-co.in.
