Privacy & Health Data Protection

Statutory Compliance: Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 • Himachal Pradesh, India

01

Physician-Patient Data Confidentiality

The Reset Co. treats all medical intake questionnaires, pulse diagnostic logs (Nadi Pariksha), constitutional dosha assessments, and tailored dietary regimens as privileged clinical health data.

Your health information is accessed solely by our licensed founding Vaidyas, Dr. Himanshu Bhatt and Dr. Aditya Kaundal (B.A.M.S.), and authorized resident attending staff directly involved in your daily therapeutic care. We enforce an uncompromising ethical and statutory rule: Your health and personal data is never leased, sold, monetized, or shared with commercial wellness aggregators, insurance intermediaries, or marketing third parties.

02

Information We Collect & Purpose Limitation

In accordance with Section 6 of the DPDP Act 2023, data is gathered strictly for legitimate, specified purposes necessary for sanctuary cohort curation and clinical safety:

  • Contact Identification: Full name, verified WhatsApp telephone number, email address, and country/city of residence (used for cohort reservations, flight arrival liaison, and doctor coordination).
  • Health & Biological Disclosures: Blood pressure status, spinal or orthopedic injuries, recent surgeries, cardiovascular history, active prescription medications, allergies, and dietary intolerances (mandatory to ensure Shatkarma, Bahya Basti, and yoga asanas are biochemically safe for you).
  • Constitutional Dosha Profiles: Prakriti analysis scores derived voluntarily through our diagnostic assessment tool.
Purpose Limitation: We never collect extraneous personal data or run unauthorized background telemetry. Every data point collected has a direct clinical or hospitality safety rationale.
03

Authorized Data Processors & Infrastructure

To operate our boutique sanctuary reservations securely, The Reset Co. engages enterprise cloud service providers acting as Data Processors under Section 8 of the DPDP Act 2023:

  • Encrypted Database & Intake Storage: Supabase Inc. (ISO 27001 / SOC 2 compliant database infrastructure).
  • Web Application & CDN Infrastructure: Vercel Inc. (TLS 1.3 encrypted edge delivery network).
  • Concierge & Doctor Communications: Meta Platforms / WhatsApp Business (end-to-end encrypted direct messaging between you and our Vaidyas).

All engaged processors are bound by strict data processing agreements prohibiting them from utilizing your personal or health data for secondary commercial purposes, advertisement targeting, or training public artificial intelligence models.

04

Data Storage, Encryption & Security Safeguards

In adherence to Section 8(5) of the DPDP Act 2023, The Reset Co. implements robust technical and organizational safeguards to prevent unauthorized access, accidental alteration, or disclosure of personal data:

  • All intake transmissions are protected using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3).
  • Stored records are encrypted at rest with AES-256 standard encryption algorithms.
  • Strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Only the founding Vaidyas and primary concierge retain authenticated credentials.
  • We do not maintain unencrypted physical paper filing cabinets at our properties; physical diagnostic notes taken during pulse readings are digitized into encrypted records and shred-disposed.
05

Data Retention, 30-Day Health Erasure & Statutory Records

In accordance with Section 8(7) of the DPDP Act 2023, personal data is retained only for the duration necessary to satisfy its initial processing purpose, distinguished across three clear tiers:

  • Confidential Health & Intake Records (30-Day Erasure): Detailed medical history, pulse notes, and diagnostic forms are retained solely during your residential retreat and for a 30-day post-retreat continuity period to support your at-home dietary and lifestyle integration. Following this 30-day window, raw health records are permanently expunged from our active operational databases.
  • Statutory Financial & Tax Records: In compliance with the Indian Income Tax Act 1961, GST Act, and commercial accounting regulations, financial records (invoices, receipts, guest name, GST numbers, and payment transaction IDs) are legally preserved for statutory audit periods (up to 8 fiscal years).
  • Alumni Relationship & Newsletter Updates: With your explicit consent, your primary contact details (name, email, WhatsApp number) are maintained in our alumni directory to keep you informed of future sanctuary cohorts, seasonal Ayurvedic guidance, and reunion invitations until you withdraw consent.
06

Anonymized Clinical Outcomes & Institutional Credibility

Under the DPDP Act 2023, anonymized data is explicitly excluded from the definition of personal data as it cannot be tied to any identifiable individual.

To establish scientific credibility, document Ayurvedic efficacy, and contribute to authentic wellness research, The Reset Co. extracts aggregated, completely de-identified outcome statistics (e.g., "88% of cohort participants experienced sustained sleep continuity improvement" or "mean reduction in perceived nervous fatigue scores").

Any guest reflection, case study, or testimonial showcased on our website or literature (such as on our reflections page) is published strictly with the guest's voluntary, affirmative written consent, honoring their preferred name format (e.g., first name, initials, or full attribution).

07

Your Rights as a Data Principal (DPDP Act 2023)

As a Data Principal under Indian law (Sections 11 & 12), you are entitled to exercise the following statutory rights:

  • Right to Access: Request a summary of the personal and health data we process about you and the identities of any authorized processors.
  • Right to Correction & Completion: Request the correction, completion, or updating of any inaccurate or outdated information.
  • Right to Erasure: Request the permanent deletion of your personal records once the retreat continuity period has concluded.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw your consent at any time by written notice. (Note: Withdrawing consent prior to or during an active retreat may necessitate modifying or pausing specific medical therapies to ensure guest safety).

To exercise any of these rights, email our privacy desk directly at privacy@thereset-co.in.

08

Right to Nominate (DPDP Section 14)

Under Section 14 of the DPDP Act 2023, you have the statutory right to nominate another individual who, in the event of your death or physical/mental incapacity, shall exercise your data protection rights on your behalf.

You may submit your nomination details (nominee's full name, relationship, and verified contact details) at any time during your intake consultation or via email to privacy@thereset-co.in.

Statutory Escalation to the Data Protection Board of India

In accordance with Section 5(1)(ii) of the DPDP Act 2023, we hereby inform you of your statutory right to escalate any grievance to the regulatory authority:

If you have lodged a privacy grievance with our Grievance Redressal Officer and have not received a response within 30 days, or if you find the resolution unsatisfactory, you have the legal right under the Act to file a complaint directly with the Data Protection Board of India (DPBI) via its official digital registry or designated portal.

11

संक्षिप्त डीपीडीपी गोपनीयता सूचना (Hindi Summary • Sec 5(3))

डिजिटल व्यक्तिगत डेटा संरक्षण अधिनियम (DPDP Act 2023) के तहत मुख्य बिंदु:

1. उद्देश्य: आपकी व्यक्तिगत एवं स्वास्थ्य संबंधी जानकारी (जैसे नाड़ी परीक्षण, स्वास्थ्य इतिहास) केवल आपकी शारीरिक सुरक्षा एवं व्यक्तिगत आयुर्वेदिक चिकित्सा के लिए ली जाती है।

2. पूर्ण गोपनीयता: आपकी जानकारी किसी भी तृतीय पक्ष, बीमा कंपनी या विज्ञापनदाता को कभी नहीं बेची जाती। केवल हमारे संस्थापक वैद्य ही इसे देख सकते हैं।

3. डेटा निष्कासन: रिट्रीट पूरा होने और 30 दिनों के अनुवर्ती मार्गदर्शन के बाद, आपकी विस्तृत नैदानिक स्वास्थ्य जानकारी स्थायी रूप से मिटा दी जाती है।

4. आपके अधिकार: आपको अपना डेटा देखने, सुधारने, मिटाने या अपनी सहमति वापस लेने का पूरा कानूनी अधिकार है।

5. शिकायत निवारण: किसी भी प्रश्न या शिकायत के लिए आप हमारे शिकायत निवारण अधिकारी को grievance@thereset-co.in पर लिख सकते हैं। 30 दिनों में समाधान न होने पर आपको भारतीय डेटा संरक्षण बोर्ड (DPBI) के समक्ष शिकायत दर्ज करने का विधिक अधिकार है।
