Prakriti Parikshan

In Ayurveda, no two bodies are identical. Before prescribing any therapeutic procedure or retreat regimen, our BAMS physicians map your inborn constitutional balance of Vata, Pitta, and Kapha.

Vata
Ether • Air
Governs movement, nervous impulses, breath, and mental agility. Prone to dry skin, cold hands, restlessness, and sleep fragmentation.
Pitta
Fire • Water
Governs metabolic transformation, digestion, intellect, and body heat. Prone to acidity, inflammation, impatience, and intense hunger.
Kapha
Water • Earth
Governs physical stability, lubrication, immunity, and endurance. Prone to sluggish digestion, fluid retention, weight gain, and lethargy.
