The Deep Rejuvenation

Natural, soothing relief for neck and back stiffness, long desk hours, and tired muscles through gentle Bahya Basti, full-body Abhyanga, and doctor-guided morning cleansing.

Targeted Neck, Back & Body Comfort

Prolonged sitting, laptop hours, and daily stress create tightness in the neck, shoulders, and lower back, leaving the body feeling stiff and fatigued.

The Deep Rejuvenation pathway is designed to gently release this stiffness and restore comfort. Under the personal care of Dr. Himanshu Bhatt and Dr. Aditya Kaundal, you receive three intensive warm herbal oil pool sessions (3 Days Bahya Basti) targeting the lumbar and cervical spine, alongside full-body relaxing Abhyanga, authentic Netra Tarpana (warm Triphala Ghrita ocular bath), hands-on Himalayan pottery, Tibetan sound healing, and an evening acoustic jamming session.

Supported by doctor-guided gentle morning cleansing, comforting grain broths, grounding pottery, and peaceful mountain walks in Himachal Pradesh.

Therapies, Practices & Sanctuary Inclusions

3 Days Prescribed Bahya Basti (Kati & Greeva Basti)

Intensive dough reservoirs shaped over the lower back (Kati Basti) and cervical spine (Greeva Basti) with continuous warm, herb-infused oil pooling. Releases muscular rigidity, relaxes back and neck tissues, and eases prolonged desk-related posture fatigue.

* Clinical Note: Classical localized therapy using authentic medicated botanical oils tailored daily by resident Vaidyas.

Netra Tarpana (Triphala Ghrita Medicated Eye Bath)

Classical ocular rejuvenation where delicate dams of black gram dough surround the eyes, filled with warm, liquid Triphala Ghrita (medicated cow's ghee). Deeply lubricates fatigued optic nerves, dissipates screen fatigue, and sharpens visual clarity.

* Authentic Botanical Protocol: Administered under sterile conditions in accordance with Caraka Samhita standards.

Mindful Himalayan Pottery & Tibetan Sound Healing

Tactile grounding workshop shaping local Kangra terracotta clay alongside traditional potters, paired with evening Tibetan singing bowl sound frequency healing to harmonize cellular resonance and deepen nervous decompression.

* Somatic Immersion: Harmonizes tactile focus and stimulates parasympathetic recovery.

Acoustic Music Jamming Session & Synchronized Abhyanga

Soulful evening unplugged acoustic music jamming circle around the outdoor pine fire pit, balanced with restorative full-body warm herbal Abhyanga massage to facilitate lymphatic drainage and muscular ease.

* Restorative Care: Integrates creative communal warmth with therapeutic bodily relaxation.

5-Day Post-Retreat Care & Guidance Handover

Individualized 5-day post-retreat home integration plan with dietary advice, daily Dinacharya schedule, and 1 virtual follow-up doctor consultation via WhatsApp concierge.

* Support: Doctor WhatsApp concierge access and follow-up support provided for 5 days.
