# Brag Plan: The Reset Co

## What is this app?
An earnest Himalayan Ayurvedic sanctuary and digital booking platform in Bir Billing designed for profound nervous system recovery.

## The angle
Ancient Ayurvedic medicine meets uncompromising clinical rigor in the high Himalayas. Strictly 15 guests per retreat, 2 resident BAMS Ayurvedic doctors, and circadian Dinacharya rituals at 2,400 meters elevation.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A slow dawn reveal across the snow-capped Dhauladhar range with the title "The Reset Co" emerging in antique gold serif typography over deep royal navy.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The Sanctuary Elevation: Bir Billing, Himachal Pradesh (2,400m altitude) with the strict 15-guest exclusivity counter.
- The 4 Sacred Disciplines: Classical Hatha, Dhyana Meditation, Shatkarma Detox, and Basti Therapy.
- Circadian Dinacharya Rhythm: From Brahma Muhurta dawn meditation to evening Trataka candle gaze.
