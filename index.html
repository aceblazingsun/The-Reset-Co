What we practise

The four classical pillars

Every retreat rests on four classical pillars. Each discipline is a complete science, practiced under direct doctor supervision.

Condition-Adaptive Yoga

Hatha • Pranayama • Restorative Asana

Classical Hatha yoga taught as a restorative therapy, not an exhausting workout. Practice in fresh mountain air facing the Dhauladhar range. Every posture, breath cycle, and spinal release is tailored to your joint health and comfort.

Vedic Dhyana & Yoga Nidra

Dharana • Trataka • Deep Nervous Reset

Guided mindfulness rooted in classical yogic science: steady concentration (Dharana), candle gazing (Trataka), and evening Yoga Nidra. These gentle practices calm the nervous system and restore deep, healthy sleep patterns.

Classical Shatkarma Purification

Jal Neti • Kapalabhati • Trataka

Traditional internal cleansing kriyas from classical Hatha yoga. Guests practice gentle nasal cleansing (Jal Neti), rhythmic breathwork (Kapalabhati), and candle gazing (Trataka). These rituals clear respiratory passages, remove morning sluggishness, and sharpen mental focus.

Localized Bahya Bastis

Kati Basti • Greeva Basti • Nabhi Basti • Netra Tarpana

Authentic warm herbal oil reservoirs held in handcrafted dough rings. Kati Basti relieves lower back tension, Greeva Basti eases neck strain, and Nabhi Basti balances the core. Completed with soothing Netra Tarpana ghee pools for screen fatigue.

Ayurvedic Constitutional Assessment

Discover your Prakriti

Prakriti Parikshan is the Ayurvedic assessment of your natural mind-body constitution. It reveals the unique balance of Vata, Pitta, & Kapha you were born with. This balance shapes your metabolism, energy levels, and individualized retreat care.

Vata

Air • Space

Governs bodily movement, nerve conduction, and respiration. In natural balance: quick-minded and creative. Under persistent stress: manifests as insomnia, joint stiffness, and racing thoughts.

Pitta

Fire • Water

Governs digestive heat, metabolic assimilation, and cognitive focus. In natural balance: discerning and decisive. When aggravated by heat: manifests as acid reflux, systemic inflammation, and mental exhaustion.

Kapha

Water • Earth

Governs physical structure, mucosal lubrication, and emotional grounding. In natural balance: calm and deeply enduring. When aggravated: manifests as fluid retention, physical heaviness, and sluggish digestion.
