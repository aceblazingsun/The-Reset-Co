What we practise

The four classical pillars

Every Reset Co retreat is built on four classical disciplines, each a complete science in its own right, harmonized under direct physician supervision.

Condition-Adaptive Yoga
Hatha • Pranayama • Restorative Asana

Classical Hatha yoga taught as a restorative therapeutic system, not an exhausting workout. Morning sessions held in crisp mountain air facing the Dhauladhar range. Breath, alignment, and gentle spinal releases tailored strictly to each guest's medical history.

Vedic Dhyana & Yoga Nidra
Dharana • Trataka • Deep Nervous Reset

Guided inward practices rooted in classical yogic science: Dharana (steady concentration), gentle Trataka (candle gazing), and therapeutic evening Yoga Nidra. Designed to quiet autonomic stress responses and restore deep, restorative circadian sleep cycles.

Classical Shatkarma Purification
Jal Neti • Kapalabhati • Trataka

The classical internal cleansing kriyas of traditional Hatha yoga. Guests are guided through gentle nasal irrigation (Jal Neti), rhythmic respiratory breathwork (Kapalabhati), and candle gazing (Trataka) to cleanse respiratory pathways, clear nocturnal stagnation, and heighten mental clarity.

Localized Panchakarma Bastis
Kati Basti • Greeva Basti • Nabhi Basti • Netra Tarpana

Authentic localized warm oil reservoirs crafted with black gram dough pools. Kati Basti targets lumbar tension, Greeva Basti relieves cervical strain, and Nabhi Basti restores solar plexus balance. Accompanied by specialized ocular care: Netra Tarpana and soothing Ashchyotana.

Ayurvedic Constitutional Assessment

Discover your Prakriti

Prakriti Parikshan is the Ayurvedic clinical diagnosis of your innate psycho-somatic constitution: the proportion of Vata, Pitta, and Kapha present from birth that governs your physical metabolism, nervous system, and digestive fire.

Vata

Air • Space

Governs kinetic movement, nerve impulses, and respiration. When harmonious: quick, creative, and enthusiastic. When aggravated by stress: leads to insomnia, dry skin, joint stiffness, and racing anxious thoughts.

Pitta

Fire • Water

Governs metabolic transformation, digestion, and intellect. When harmonious: sharp, discerning, and decisive. When aggravated by heat and overwork: manifests as inflammation, hyperacidity, irritability, and burnout.

Kapha

Water • Earth

Governs biological structure, lubrication, and emotional resilience. When harmonious: grounded, calm, and deeply loving. When aggravated: manifests as fluid retention, physical heaviness, metabolic sluggishness, and fatigue.

Daily Retreat Flow

The rhythm of your days

Rather than watching the clock, every retreat flows through a natural biological progression designed around the classical Ayurvedic science of Dinacharya.

01 • Dawn Waking
Morning Dinacharya & Hydration

Waking gently to crisp mountain air. Warm water resting overnight in your copper bottle, classical tongue scraping to clear oral toxins, and quiet grounding before sunrise.

02 • Morning Practice
Condition-Adaptive Yoga & Pranayama

Restorative classical Hatha asanas and conscious breathwork practiced outdoors facing the Dhauladhar peaks, tailored to spinal comfort and joint ease.

03 • Nourishment
Organic Sattvic Breakfast

Warm organic grains, stewed seasonal mountain fruits, and freshly prepared herbal decoctions served in quiet mindfulness.

04 • Clinical Consultation
Physician Evaluation & Shatkarma

Individual medical intake with Dr. Himanshu and Dr. Aditya. Instruction and guided practice of classical Jal Neti and gentle Kapalabhati purification.

05 • Midday Pause
Sattvic Lunch & Unhurried Rest

Wholesome, easily digestible meal followed by dedicated downtime: quiet reading, pine terrace contemplation, or gentle unhurried walking.

06 • Panchakarma
Prescribed Bastis & Ocular Care

Direct physician-administered warm oil reservoirs (Kati, Greeva, or Nabhi Basti) paired with restorative Netra Tarpana ghee eye pools and soothing Ashchyotana.

07 • Experiential Discovery
Pottery Workshop & Pine Nature Trek

Tactile clay grounding sessions with local Himalayan artisans and guided mindful treks through fragrant pine trails and mountain monastery paths.

08 • Night Reflection
Stargazing, Fireside Gathering & Yoga Nidra

Evening Sattvic supper, contemplation under clear mountain skies, acoustic fireside conversations, and deep nervous system restoration with guided Yoga Nidra.
