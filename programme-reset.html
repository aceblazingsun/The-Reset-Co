Therapies, Practices & Important Details

3 Warm Oil Bahya Basti Sessions

Warm herbal oil gently held in place over areas like your lower back (Kati Basti), neck (Greeva Basti), or knees (Janu Basti) to relieve stiffness, soothe tired joints, and bring lasting comfort.

* Special Care: Traditional dough dams are prepared fresh on-site with organic flour. Warmth is carefully maintained throughout the session for deep relaxation.

Netra Tarpana & Triphala Kwath Eye Wash

Gentle traditional eye care featuring a soothing Triphala Kwath eye wash and warm herbal compresses to relieve screen fatigue, dry eyes, and mental strain from digital overload.

* Thoughtful Care: Each session is tailored comfortably. If eyes are sensitive, calming cranial Marma therapy is gently offered as an alternative.

Restorative Marma Therapy

Gentle, focused pressure along vital energy points (Marma) to release accumulated muscle tension, quiet an overactive nervous system, and restore natural energy flow.

* Personal Care: Tailored to your specific areas of tension and relaxation needs.

Doctor-Guided Shatkarma Cleansing

Gentle morning cleansing with Jalaneti and Kapalabhati, followed by morning silence until breakfast and nourishing warm broths to kindle digestive fire.

* Guidance: Guided step-by-step with the doctors present to ensure complete comfort.

Daily 1:1 Doctor Interaction & 7-Day Dinacharya Handover

Dedicated daily one-on-one interaction with Dr. Himanshu Bhatt and Dr. Aditya Kaundal to monitor your daily progress, digestion, and comfort, concluding on the final day with a personalized 7-day Dinacharya routine handover for home.

* Continuing Guidance: Includes personal WhatsApp support for 7 days post-retreat to help you ease smoothly back into your routine.
