Sanctuary Code of Conduct

The Six Conduct Pillars

A sanctuary is not a hotel. It is a shared container of biological restoration and psychological silence, held in sacred trust by 12 guests and our founding physicians.

The clinical therapies of classical Ayurveda (Bahya Basti, Shatkarma, and Dinacharya) depend upon autonomic nervous system down-regulation. When an environment is chaotic, loud, or distracted by screens, the cortisol response remains elevated, blunting herbal absorption and digestive Agni. By joining a Reset Co. cohort, every guest pledges commitment to the following six sacred pillars.

01

Digital Sundown & Sensory Fasting

Smartphones, laptops, and tablets are kept inside private room sanctums during morning kriyas, communal dining, and evening silence hours. Communal garden terraces and therapy suites remain completely screen-free zones.

Protocol: Emergency contact numbers are provided to family members via the Concierge desk.
02

Noble Silence Mornings (Arya Mauna)

From waking until the conclusion of morning Hatha yoga and digestive breakfast, guests maintain noble silence. Speech consumes subtle nervous energy (Prana); morning stillness allows internal vital force to gather without dissipation.

Protocol: Urgent physical needs are communicated quietly to attending staff or physicians.
03

Egalitarian Sanctuary Vastra

Upon arrival, every guest receives two sets of handcrafted pure cotton Sanctuary Vastra in their Welcome Kit. Wearing simple, breathable white cotton during therapies and meals eliminates status comparison and restores natural dignity.

Protocol: All guests dress uniformly, creating a supportive, non-competitive community atmosphere.
04

Purity of Nourishment (Ahara Shuddhi)

All food served is freshly prepared Sattvic cuisine, tailored to the clinical stages of Samsarjana Krama. To protect metabolic Agni and Shatkarma detox protocols, zero outside food, commercial packaged snacks, caffeine, alcohol, or nicotine are permitted on property.

Protocol: 100% smoke-free, alcohol-free, and commercial-snack-free sanctuary perimeter.
05

Barefoot Grounding (Prithvi Sparsha)

Designated grassy meadows and outdoor yoga shalas are barefoot zones. Direct skin contact with Himalayan soil discharges accumulated static charges, neutralizes oxidative stress, and grounds erratic Vata dosha.

Protocol: Footwear is set aside at the entrance to therapy rooms and meditation gazebos.
06

Sacred Decorum & Nature Reverence

Our Himalayan valley is home to ancient deodar pines and mountain wildlife. Guests speak in gentle conversational tones, walk mindfully on forest trails, and refrain from personal loud music (our retreat team utilizes acoustic speakers solely for sacred Om chanting and soothing meditative soundscapes). Commercial filming is permitted with prior coordination, honoring guest privacy during clinical therapies.

Protocol: Preserving the mountain stillness for both fellow guests and native mountain wildlife.
