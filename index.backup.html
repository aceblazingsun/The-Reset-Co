What we practise

The four pillars

Every Reset Co retreat is built on four classical disciplines, each one a complete science in its own right, more powerful when practised together under medical supervision.

Yoga
Hatha · Pranayama · Asana · Sun Salutation

Classical Hatha yoga taught as a complete system, not a fitness class. Morning practices at altitude in Bir Billing, with the Dhauladhar range as your horizon. Breath, posture, and awareness taught in their classical sequence by BAMS-trained practitioners who understand the body medically.

Meditation
Dhyana · Dharana · Trataka · Yoga Nidra

Guided meditation rooted in the Yoga Sutras of Patanjali: Dharana (concentration), Dhyana (sustained meditation), and Trataka (gazing) practiced in sequence. Not mindfulness as trend, but as clinical technology for nervous system restoration.

Shatkarma
Neti · Dhauti · Nauli · Basti · Kapalabhati · Trataka

The six classical purification practices of Hatha yoga. Supervised by Dr. Himanshu and Dr. Aditya, these kriyas prepare the body and nervous system for deeper yogic practice. Rarely taught with full medical oversight. We teach them correctly and safely.

Ayurvedic Procedure
Janu Basti · Greeva Basti · Kati Basti · Hriday Basti · Yakrut Basti

Classical Ayurvedic Basti procedures prescribed and administered by BAMS doctors. Janu Basti for knee and joint care, Greeva Basti for cervical relief, Kati Basti for lower back restoration, Hriday Basti for cardiac vitality, and Yakrut Basti for liver and digestive support. Each procedure is selected based on your individual Prakriti and current health needs.

Ayurvedic Constitutional Assessment

Know your Prakriti

Prakriti Parikshan is the Ayurvedic science of understanding your inborn constitution: the unique blend of Vata, Pitta, and Kapha that shapes your body, mind, and health tendencies. Knowing your Prakriti allows our BAMS doctors to personalise every aspect of your retreat.

Vata
Air & Space

Quick, creative, and enthusiastic. When in balance: inspired and adaptive. When disturbed: anxious, scattered, and depleted.

Pitta
Fire & Water

Sharp, driven, and passionate. When in balance: focused and courageous. When disturbed: inflamed, irritable, and burning out.

Kapha
Earth & Water

Strong, calm, and enduring. When in balance: nurturing and loyal. When disturbed: lethargic, heavy, and resistant to change.

30 Questions · 8 Classical Domains · 6–8 Minutes

Our 30-question clinical assessment covers Sharira Prakriti, Twacha & Kesha, Agni, Nidra, Manas, stress responses, energy, and climate adaptation (the eight domains of classical Ayurvedic examination).
