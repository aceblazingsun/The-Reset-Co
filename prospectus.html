Our foundation

Philosophy & Vision

The Reset Co was founded on a simple belief: that the most sophisticated healing systems in the world are already thousands of years old — they simply need to be delivered with clinical integrity, genuine care, and the right environment.

Situated in the Himalayan valley of Bir Billing — India's paragliding capital, a UNESCO-recognised paragliding site, and one of the most spiritually alive landscapes in northern India — our retreats are not aesthetic escapes. They are medically guided programmes, designed by BAMS-qualified Ayurvedic doctors, and delivered in intimate groups of no more than 15 guests to ensure every individual receives real attention.

We collaborate with carefully selected boutique hotels in Bir Billing to deliver experiences that are deeply local and authentically Himalayan. We do not own a building. We own a practice.

What we practise

The Four Pillars

🧘
Yoga
Hatha · Pranayama · Asana

Classical Hatha yoga taught as a complete system — posture, breath, and awareness in unified sequence. Surya Namaskar (12-step sun salutation) as a meditative moving prayer. All yoga sessions are delivered facing the Dhauladhar range in Bir Billing, at an altitude that naturally deepens the breath.

  • Surya Namaskar (Sun Salutation) — 12 classical steps
  • Standing asana series: Warrior, Triangle, Tree sequences
  • Floor asanas: Cobra, Boat, Bridge, Spinal Twists
  • Pranayama: Anulom-Vilom, Bhramari, Bhastrika, Kumbhaka
  • Yoga Nidra: deep yogic sleep practice
  • Restorative Yin for post-treatment integration
🕯️
Meditation
Dharana · Dhyana · Trataka · Yoga Nidra

Guided meditation from the Yoga Sutras of Patanjali — the original scientific text of meditative psychology. We teach the complete arc: from Dharana (single-pointed focus) through Dhyana (sustained meditation) toward Samadhi (integration). Dr. Aditya's Patanjali training ensures this is taught authentically.

  • Dharana: object, breath, mantra, and visualisation techniques
  • Dhyana: progressive unguided sitting (5min → 45min across retreat)
  • Trataka: candle and flame gazing — improves concentration, treats anxiety
  • Yoga Nidra: 45-75 minute deep relaxation guided by a doctor
  • Constitution-specific mantra (Transformation programme)
💧
Shatkarma
The Six Classical Purification Practices

Shatkarma (six actions) are the classical purification practices of Hatha yoga, described in the Hatha Yoga Pradipika. They prepare the body for deeper practice by removing physical impurities. Rarely taught correctly outside certified Ayurvedic clinical settings — we teach them with full medical supervision.

  • Jala Neti — saline nasal irrigation; clears sinuses, improves breathing
  • Sutra Neti — rubber catheter technique (advanced)
  • Kapalabhati — rapid exhale breathing; detoxifies lungs, activates core
  • Nauli — abdominal churning; massages digestive organs, improves agni
  • Dhauti — upper digestive tract cleansing
  • Trataka — sustained gazing; strengthens concentration and vision
🌿
Ayurvedic Treatment
Abhyanga · Shirodhara · Panchakarma

All treatments are prescribed following a genuine BAMS Ayurvedic consultation — not a wellness menu. Your prakriti (constitution) and current Vikruti (imbalance) determine which treatments you receive. No two guests receive identical treatment programmes.

  • Abhyanga — full-body warm herbal oil massage; 60-75 minutes
  • Shirodhara — warm oil continuously poured over the forehead; 60-90 min
  • Udvartana — herbal powder massage; reduces Kapha, stimulates circulation
  • Kati Basti — warm oil pooled on lower back; for spinal and kidney health
  • Nasya — medicated nasal oil administration
  • Panchakarma protocols — as prescribed by consultation
