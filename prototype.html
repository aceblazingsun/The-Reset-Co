The Approach

Ancient medicine.
Actual doctors.

Most wellness retreats offer a version of relaxation with Ayurvedic packaging. We offer something different: a medically supervised programme designed by two practising physicians who have spent their careers in classical Panchakarma.

Dr. Himanshu Sharma and Dr. Aditya Sharma conduct individual medical intakes with every guest before any therapy begins. Your programme is built around your constitution, your clinical history, and your specific conditions — not a standard menu.

Everything is done at altitude in Bir Billing, Himachal Pradesh, where the mountain air, organic food supply, and natural rhythm of the landscape are part of the treatment itself.

The Rhythm of Your Days

What your days look like

01 • Dawn Waking
Morning Dinacharya & Hydration

Waking gently to crisp mountain air. Warm water from your copper bottle, classical tongue scraping to clear oral toxins, and quiet grounding before sunrise.

02 • Morning Practice
Condition-Adaptive Yoga & Pranayama

Restorative classical Hatha asanas and conscious breathwork practiced outdoors facing the Dhauladhar peaks, tailored to spinal comfort and joint ease.

03 • Nourishment
Organic Sattvic Breakfast

Warm organic grains, stewed seasonal mountain fruits, and freshly prepared herbal decoctions served in quiet mindfulness.

04 • Clinical Consultation
Physician Evaluation & Shatkarma

Individual medical intake with Dr. Himanshu and Dr. Aditya. Instruction and guided practice of classical Jal Neti and gentle Kapalabhati purification.

05 • Midday Pause
Sattvic Lunch & Unhurried Rest

Wholesome, easily digestible meal followed by dedicated downtime — quiet reading, pine terrace contemplation, or gentle unhurried walking.

06 • Panchakarma
Prescribed Bastis & Ocular Care

Physician-administered warm oil reservoirs (Kati, Greeva, or Nabhi Basti) paired with restorative Netra Tarpana ghee eye pools and soothing Ashchyotana.

07 • Experiential Discovery
Pottery Workshop & Pine Nature Trek

Tactile clay grounding sessions with local Himalayan artisans and guided mindful treks through fragrant pine trails and mountain monastery paths.

08 • Night Reflection
Stargazing, Fireside & Acoustic Music

Evening Sattvic supper, contemplation under clear mountain skies, acoustic fireside conversations, and soothing post-dinner music under the stars.
