Our Scope of Care & Safety Standard

Educational & Restorative Scope: The Reset Co. conducts residential wellness retreats designed for deep relaxation, circadian reset, and constitutional balance. We do not operate an inpatient hospital, and our retreats are not intended to diagnose, treat, or cure acute medical illnesses.

Individual Assessment: Every prospective guest completes a confidential health suitability review prior to booking. Certain external therapies (such as specific Bahya Basti applications) are customized or modified based on individual comfort and medical history.

Emergency Procedures: While our founding doctors reside on property throughout the residency, local regional medical facilities in Bir and Palampur are mapped and accessible for any unforeseen medical needs.
