Terms of Service & Sanctuary Protocol

Clear medical disclosures, retreat reservation ethics, and mutual commitments for our doctor-led cohorts.

The Sanctuary Accord (7 Core Commitments)

Review the essential commitments regarding noble silence, zero intoxicants, sensory fasting, and wellness discretion before booking.

01

Sanctuary Scope & Medical Disclaimer

The Reset Co. operates doctor-led classical Ayurvedic residential retreat cohorts in Himachal Pradesh under the professional guidance of licensed BAMS physicians Dr. Himanshu Bhatt and Dr. Aditya Kaundal.

Our retreats provide restorative health optimization, classical Dinacharya, physician-supervised Shatkarma, and localized Bahya Basti. The sanctuary is not a hospital or acute medical facility. We do not treat active surgical emergencies, critical psychiatric crises, unstable cardiac conditions, or communicable acute infections. Guests requiring emergency medical intervention must seek hospital care.

Ayurvedic Philosophy: We practice in accordance with the classical Sanskrit principle स्वस्थस्य स्वास्थ्यरक्षणम् आतुरस्य विकारप्रशमनं च — preserving the health of the healthy and alleviating disorders through restorative balance.
02

Health Disclosures & Voluntary Participation

Every prospective guest undergoes a confidential pre-retreat intake consultation. Guests agree to fully disclose their complete medical history, including current prescription medications, allergies, spinal surgeries, and cardiovascular conditions.

Participation in yoga asanas, breathwork, Shatkarma cleansing kriyas, and herbal therapies is voluntary. Our attending physicians retain full professional discretion to modify, pause, or substitute any procedure (such as substituting Bahya Basti with restorative Abhyanga) if deemed in the guest's best biological interest.

03

Reservations, Rescheduling & Refund Policy

Due to the intimate 8–10 guest capacity (5 earthen suites) of each cohort and fixed boutique sanctuary property commitments:

  • Deposit: A ₹10,000 reservation deposit secures your cohort placement, with the remaining balance due 15 days prior to check-in (for bookings made within 15 days of the retreat commencement, 100% payment is required upon confirmation).
  • 15+ Days Prior: Cancellations requested 15 days or more prior to retreat commencement receive a 100% refund less a 5% administrative payment processing fee.
  • Within 14 Days: Cancellations requested within 14 days of retreat commencement are non-refundable. However, 100% of the funds paid may be transferred toward any future Reset Co. cohort within 3 months (90 days).
Capacity & Rescheduling: Retreat cohorts are strictly capped at 8–10 guests across 5 suites to preserve personal Vaidya attention. In the rare event a cohort must be rescheduled due to severe weather or operational constraints, guests receive at least 14 days' notice with the choice of a 100% immediate refund or a complimentary tier upgrade into any cohort within 3 months.
04

Sanctuary Code of Conduct & Zero Intoxicants

To preserve communal tranquility and protect digestive Agni, all guests must abide by the Sanctuary Conduct Pillars (Digital Sundown, Noble Silence mornings, and Sattvic dining). Commercial smoking, alcohol consumption, illicit narcotics, or disruptive verbal behavior are strictly prohibited. The Reset Co. reserves the right to discharge any individual who violates these rules without refund.

Sensory Fasting: Smartphones and laptops remain in private room sanctums during morning practices, communal dining, and evening hours. Therapy suites, gardens, and dining halls are screen-free zones.
05

Acoustic Environment & Filming Policy

Acoustic Environment: Personal loud music and guest-operated portable speakers are strictly prohibited across all rooms, yoga spaces, and gardens. To support deep nervous system restoration, the retreat faculty is authorized to use dedicated acoustic speakers for sacred Om chanting, Vedic recitations, and soothing meditative sounds during scheduled yoga sessions and quiet dining hours.

Filming & Photography: Commercial filming and professional photography are not barred on sanctuary grounds, provided prior operational clearance is coordinated with sanctuary leadership and private guest wellness consultations remain strictly confidential and unrecorded.

06

Governing Law & Exclusive Jurisdiction

These terms and all retreat agreements are governed by the laws of India. Any legal disputes arising out of sanctuary stays shall fall under the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts of Himachal Pradesh, India.
