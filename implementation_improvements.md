## Executive Summary

The objective of this roadmap is to elevate the perceived value, clinical authority, and guest satisfaction of The Reset Co without enlarging the operational model, hiring additional management staff, or incurring capital expenditure. 

By restructuring specific touchpoints into intentional clinical rituals, we transform standard hospitality offerings into signature Ayurvedic experiences that generate exceptional organic word of mouth and high retention.

## 1. The Dinacharya Welcome Caddy (Arrival Ritual)

### The Concept
Replace standard hotel check-in with a tangible Ayurvedic arrival gift placed on the guest's bed upon arrival.

### Bill of Materials & Cost Breakdown
* **Hand-Cast Pure Copper Tongue Scraper:** Cost: ~₹110. (Eliminates overnight Ama/oral endotoxins).
* **Cold-Pressed Sesame Oil Dropper Bottle (50ml):** Cost: ~₹90. (Infused with a trace of camphor for morning Gandusha/oil pulling).
* **Pure Triphala Eye Wash & Digestive Powder Tin (50g):** Cost: ~₹70. (Netra Shuddhi and Agni support).
* **Bound Linen Pocket Journal & Wooden Pencil:** Cost: ~₹120. (For daily reflections and doctor notes).
* **Cotton Drawstring Linen Bag with Reset Co Stamp:** Cost: ~₹60.
* **Total Cost per Guest:** ~₹450.
* **Perceived Retail Value:** ₹2,500 to ₹3,500.

### Operational Execution
1. Caddies are prepared 24 hours prior to batch arrival.
2. The boutique hotel staff places the bag directly on the bed with a handwritten welcome note signed by Dr. Aditya and Dr. Himanshu.
3. During the opening orientation, Dr. Aditya explains how each item will be used during morning Dinacharya starting at 5:30 AM the next day.
4. Guests take the complete kit home, keeping the brand and habits visible on their nightstands for months.

## 2. The Doctor-Signed Prakriti Blueprint (The Medical Moat)

### The Concept
Deliver a formal, personalized physical health blueprint on Day 4 rather than sending verbal advice or generic emails.

### Dossier Structure
1. **Prakriti Diagnostic Assessment:**
   * Primary and secondary dosha breakdown (Vata, Pitta, Kapha ratios) based on the opening pulse reading (Nadi Pariksha) and intake assessment.
2. **Nutritional Prescription Chart:**
   * A clear two-column reference: *Foods That Kindle Your Agni* versus *Foods That Provoke Your Dosha*.
   * Specific spices and kitchen remedies suited for the guest's city routine.
3. **Personalized 15-Minute Urban Morning Routine:**
   * Tailored specifically to the guest's work life (e.g. corporate desk worker, frequent flyer, high-stress manager).
4. **Three Over-The-Counter Classical Formulations:**
   * Accessible herbal recommendations easily purchased in major metros (e.g. Brahmi Vati for sleep, Ashwagandha for nervous exhaustion, Avipattikar for Pitta acidity).

### The Retention Protocol (Day 14 Follow-up)
* Each guest receives one scheduled 15-minute WhatsApp audio consultation on Day 14 after returning home.
* Purpose: Review how the transition back to city life is progressing and answer questions about their protocol.
* Strategic Outcome: This single touchpoint cements personal loyalty and prompts natural referral conversations among colleagues.

## 3. Sattvic Food Storytelling & Agni Enhancement

### The Concept
Transform meals from passive eating into an educational mindful ritual using zero-cost kitchen guidelines.

### Kitchen Guidelines for Partner Properties
* Zero refined white sugar; use raw jaggery or wildflower honey.
* Zero heavy commercial refined oils; use cold-pressed mustard, sesame, or pure cow's ghee.
* Follow Langhana principles: dinners must be lighter and earlier (completed by 7:30 PM).

### Table Explanation Cards (3 x 4 inch card at each setting)
* **Example Lunch Card:**  
  *Dish:* Organic Split Mung and Kodo Millet Khichdi with Cultured Ghee and Cumin.  
  *Ayurvedic Action:* Deepana (kindles digestive fire) and Pachana (metabolizes cellular residue).  
  *Mindful Prompt:* "Chew deliberately. Notice the six tastes (Shad Rasa) present in the meal."

### The CCF Digestive Infusion Protocol
* Serve warm CCF Tea (Cumin, Coriander, Fennel seeds steeped in mountain spring water) in copper carafes 15 minutes before lunch and dinner.
* Cleanses the palate, stimulates enzymatic secretion, and prevents post-meal sluggishness.

## 4. "Mouna" Sunrise Forest Walks (Noble Silence)

### The Concept
A 40-minute pre-dawn mindful mountain walk conducted in complete silence prior to morning Hatha yoga.

### Execution Routine
* **Time:** 6:00 AM to 6:40 AM (prior to morning asana).
* **Location:** The pine and oak trail connecting Bir to Upper Billing ridgeline.
* **Pre-Walk Rule:** All phones remain in rooms. No verbal conversation from leaving the hotel until returning to the yoga hall.
* **Why It Works:** Urban professionals rarely experience sustained auditory silence. Walking among Himalayan deodar trees without talking regulates sensory overload, lowers cortisol, and sets up a deeply receptive state for morning pranayama.
* **Direct Cost:** ₹0.

## 5. Shatkarma Physiology Briefing (Transforming Fear to Science)

### The Concept
Frame ancient yogic cleansing practices as modern physiological optimization before any physical kriya begins.

### Pre-Practice Script Outline for Doctors
1. **Demystifying Jala Neti:**
   * Explain the osmotic balance of saline water and its soothing effect on the mucous membranes.
   * Highlight how it stimulates the olfactory bulb, cooling cranial circulation and clearing mental fog.
2. **The Mechanics of Kapalabhati:**
   * Clarify that passive inhalation with active exhalation creates rhythmic diaphragmatic pumping.
   * Explain its stimulation of the vagus nerve and enhanced venous return to the heart.
3. **Safety & Autonomy:**
   * Emphasize that procedures are performed under BAMS medical supervision.
   * Reiterate that every guest progresses strictly at their own pace with full permission to pause or adjust.
