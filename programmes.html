Physician-Supervised Cleansing

The Shatkarma & Samsarjana Krama Protocol

Unlike standard commercial retreats where cleansing is left to intuition, our internal cleansing is directly supervised by a certified BAMS physician and a certified yoga practitioner, supported by classical dietary rhythms.

01

Pre-Kriya Preparation

Two days prior to the retreat, guests follow guided light Sattvic meals. Digestion is lightened, reducing heavy Ama (metabolic toxins) before arrival.

02

Physician Clinical Screen

On Day 2, our Vaidyas assess your current Agni (digestive fire), blood pressure, and vitality to customize the exact intensity of your morning Shatkarma.

03

Supervised Kriya & Liquid Rest

On Day 3 morning, Jalaneti, Kapalabhati, and gentle gut cleansing are performed under direct clinical guidance. The remainder of Day 3 is dedicated to noble silence and liquid nourishment.

04

Samsarjana Krama Rekindling

Following cleansing, Agni is gently rekindled with classical Manda, Peya, and Vilepi warm grain broths infused with cumin and pure A2 cow ghee, pacifying Vata.

Clinical Disclosures & Procedure Notes (*)

*
Bahya Basti (External Oil Pools): These therapies are external, localized reservoirs of warm medicated herbal oil (Kati Basti for lower back, Greeva Basti for neck, Janu Basti for knees, Hrid Basti for thoracic comfort). They are not invasive internal enemas. If a clinical consultation reveals you have zero musculoskeletal discomfort, our physicians will substitute these sessions with nourishing Abhyanga, Shirodhara, or sound healing therapy so your investment is always honoured with therapeutic value.
*
Netra Tarpana (Ocular Nourishment): A classical therapy where eye wells crafted from organic black gram flour are filled with warm medicated Triphala ghee. Indicated for digital screen strain, dry eyes, and nervous tension. Guests with acute eye infections or active conjunctivitis will receive cranial Marma therapy instead.
*
Shatkarma Eligibility: All internal purifications are voluntary and conducted under joint supervision by a qualified BAMS doctor and an experienced yoga teacher. Blood pressure and gastrointestinal comfort are checked prior to procedures. Guests with active ulcers, severe hypertension, or hernia are guided through gentle breathwork without vigorous kriya.
*
Uniform Room Accommodations: To maintain egalitarian community harmony and prevent status competition, all rooms across both Double and Triple Occupancy tiers are identical boutique mountain rooms in our Himalayan sanctuary partner estate. Price differences reflect therapeutic resources, physician time, and specialized herbal preparations.
