Doctor-Guided Cleansing

The Shatkarma & Samsarjana Krama Protocol

Unlike standard commercial retreats where cleansing is left to intuition, our morning cleansing is directly guided by our founding Ayurvedic doctors and experienced yoga teacher, supported by gentle dietary rhythms.

01

Pre-Kriya Preparation

Two days prior to the retreat, guests follow guided light meals. Digestion is lightened, easing metabolic load before arrival.

02

Doctor Check-in & Orientation

On Day 2, our doctors check in with you during your 1-on-1 interaction to assess your digestive comfort and vitality, personalizing your morning Shatkarma session.

03

Doctor-Guided Cleansing & Rest

On Day 3 morning, gentle Jalaneti and Kapalabhati breathwork are practiced with step-by-step guidance. Morning silence is observed until breakfast, followed by nourishing warm broths.

04

Samsarjana Krama Rekindling

Following cleansing, digestion is gently rekindled with classical warm Manda, Peya and nourishing rice broths infused with roasted cumin and mild herbs.

Therapy Notes & Important Details (*)

*
Bahya Basti (Warm Oil Pools): These therapies are external, localized pools of warm herbal oil (Kati Basti for lower back, Greeva Basti for neck, Janu Basti for knees, Hrid Basti for chest comfort). They are non-invasive and deeply relaxing. If your consultation indicates you do not have neck or back stiffness, our doctors will substitute these sessions with restorative Abhyanga, Marma therapy, or sound meditation so your care is always tailored to your comfort.
*
Netra Tarpana & Eye Care: Traditional ocular care featuring Netra Tarpana (nourishing warm Triphala Ghrita ghee bath) and soothing Netra Prakshalana (cleansing Triphala Kwath herbal wash) with herbal compresses to relieve digital screen strain, dry eyes, and mental exhaustion. Guests with sensitive eyes or active irritation will receive calming facial Marma therapy instead.
*
Shatkarma Morning Cleansing: All morning cleansing kriyas are gentle, voluntary, and guided step-by-step with our Ayurvedic doctors and yoga teacher. General comfort and digestive readiness are checked beforehand. Guests who prefer a gentler pace are guided through simple breathwork.
*
Earthen Suite Stay: Twin-sharing base tariff (Private Single Suite & Couple options available across 3 Double & 2 Twin suites). The all-inclusive tariff covers your complete stay, daily Vaidya interactions, wholesome meals, and restorative care with transparent pricing.

Guidance & Retreat Clarifications

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers on our doctor-led Himachal retreat, 8-to-10 guest cohort capacity (5 earthen suites), classical Ayurvedic therapies, and intake requirements.

Each cohort requires a minimum of 8 guests to confirm the residency, and is strictly capped at 8–10 guests across 5 private earthen suites. This deliberate boutique limit ensures our founding Ayurvedic doctors (Dr. Himanshu Bhatt and Dr. Aditya Kaundal) can conduct unhurried daily one-on-one interactions and maintain personalized oversight for every participant.

Our retreats excel for burnout, chronic digital fatigue, sluggish digestion, joint stiffness, neck and back ache, sleep disruption, and nervous exhaustion. Please note that we are a restorative classical Ayurvedic sanctuary, not an acute surgical or emergency hospital. Guests with active acute medical crises or surgical needs must seek hospital treatment.

Depending on your chosen pathway (The Awakening Pathway, The Deep Rejuvenation, or The Classical Reset), therapies include localized Bahya Basti (warm herbal oil therapies on the cervical or lumbar spine), full-body synchronized Abhyanga, deeply nourishing Netra Tarpana (warm medicated Triphala Ghrita eye bath), soothing Netra Prakshalana (cleansing Triphala Kwath eye wash), gentle morning cleansing kriyas (Jalaneti), condition-adaptive restorative yoga, and personalized herbal decoctions.

A ₹10,000 reservation deposit secures your cohort placement, with the remaining balance due 15 days prior to check-in (for bookings made within 15 days of the retreat commencement, 100% payment is required upon confirmation). Cancellations requested 15 days or more prior to check-in receive a 100% refund (less a 5% administrative payment processing fee). Cancellations requested within 14 days of arrival are non-refundable; however, 100% of all funds paid may be transferred toward any future Reset Co. cohort within 3 months (90 days).

To nurture mental tranquility, all guests observe Arya Mauna (morning noble silence) from waking until after morning yoga and breakfast. In accordance with our Digital Sundown protocol, phones and laptops remain inside your private room during morning sessions, therapies, and communal dining. Therapy suites, gardens, and dining halls are screen-free environments.

The retreat is hosted at Vaikunth Mud House, Upper Bir Khas Pine Valley, Bir Billing, Kangra (HP 176077). The closest airport is Kangra/Dharamshala Airport (DHM), approximately 1.5 hours scenic drive from the property. Daily luxury overnight Volvo buses operate directly between New Delhi (ISBT Kashmere Gate / Majnu Ka Tilla) or Chandigarh and Bir Tibetan Colony. Our concierge assists with chauffeured transfer coordination.
