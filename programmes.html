Vaidya-Supervised Cleansing

The Shatkarma & Samsarjana Krama Protocol

Unlike standard commercial retreats where cleansing is left to intuition, our internal cleansing is directly supervised by a certified Vaidya and a certified yoga practitioner, supported by classical dietary rhythms.

01

Pre-Kriya Preparation

Two days prior to the retreat, guests follow guided light Sattvic meals. Digestion is lightened, reducing heavy Ama (metabolic toxins) before arrival.

02

Physician Clinical Screen

On Day 2, our Vaidyas assess your current Agni (digestive fire), blood pressure, and vitality to customize the exact intensity of your morning Shatkarma.

03

Supervised Kriya & Liquid Rest

On Day 3 morning, Jalaneti, Kapalabhati breathwork, and physician-supervised gut cleansing are performed under direct clinical guidance. The remainder of Day 3 is dedicated to noble silence and liquid nourishment.

04

Samsarjana Krama Rekindling

Following cleansing, Agni is gently rekindled with classical Manda, Peya & nourishing Vilepi grain broths infused with cumin and pure A2 cow ghee, pacifying Vata.

Clinical Disclosures & Procedure Notes (*)

*
Bahya Basti (External Oil Pools): These therapies are external, localized reservoirs of warm medicated herbal oil (Kati Basti for lower back, Greeva Basti for neck, Janu Basti for knees, Hrid Basti for thoracic comfort). They are not invasive internal enemas. If a clinical consultation reveals you have zero musculoskeletal discomfort, our physicians will substitute these sessions with nourishing Abhyanga, Marma point therapy, or sound healing therapy so your investment is always honoured with therapeutic value.
*
Netra Tarpana (Ocular Nourishment): A classical therapy where eye wells formed from organic black gram flour are filled with warm medicated Triphala ghee. Indicated for digital screen strain, dry eyes, and nervous tension. Guests with acute eye infections or active conjunctivitis will receive cranial Marma therapy instead.
*
Shatkarma Eligibility: All internal purifications are voluntary and conducted under joint supervision by a certified Vaidya and an experienced yoga teacher. Blood pressure and gastrointestinal comfort are checked prior to procedures. Guests with active ulcers, severe hypertension, or hernia are guided through gentle breathwork without vigorous kriya.
*
Uniform Room Accommodations: To maintain egalitarian community harmony, all sanctuary rooms are peaceful boutique mountain accommodations in our Himachal sanctuary estate. The all-inclusive tariff covers your complete stay, daily doctor consultations, wholesome organic meals, and therapeutic care with transparent pricing.
Sanctuary Rhythm

The 4-Day Restorative Architecture

A synchronized biological rhythm balancing classical Ayurveda, gentle yoga, and deep Himalayan stillness.

Orientation & Grounding

Midday check-in, initial individual physician pulse and constitution consultation, distribution of the Sanctuary Kit with handcrafted cotton vastra, unhurried acclimatization, evening introductory Hatha, and warm Sattvic dinner.

Day 2 • Activation

Spine & Structural Alignment

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers on our doctor-led Himachal residency, 8-to-12 guest cohort capacity, classical Ayurvedic therapies, and intake requirements.

Each cohort requires a minimum of 8 guests to confirm the residency, and is strictly capped at 12 guests maximum. Our retreats excel for corporate burnout, chronic digital fatigue, metabolic and digestive sluggishness (Mandagni), joint stiffness, musculoskeletal back/neck ache, sleep disruption, and nervous exhaustion.
